# Calypso Workstation (Nasdaq Calypso)

> Calypso Workstation is the user-facing desktop component of the Nasdaq Calypso
> capital markets platform (formerly Adenza / Calypso Technology, acquired by
> Nasdaq, Inc. in 2023). It gives trading, risk and portfolio teams real-time
> market data, trade entry, order management, risk and P&L monitoring, scenario
> analysis and portfolio analytics across asset classes. It is an integrated
> client application, not a publicly documented API.

## Status for agents

- **No public API.** No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, gRPC/Protobuf or
  WSDL contract is published at any reachable Nasdaq or Calypso host.
- **No developer portal, no API reference, no SDKs, no CLI, no MCP server, no
  A2A agent card.** All probed and absent as of 2026-09-05.
- **No self-service sign-up and no published pricing.** Enterprise, contract
  negotiated; the only commercial route is a contact-sales form.
- **Technical documentation is customer-gated.** It sits behind the Nasdaq
  Customer Portal, which is Okta-authenticated.
- **calypso.com is dark.** The pre-acquisition domain resolves but serves nothing
  on port 80 or 443. Every live surface is now under nasdaq.com.

There is nothing here an agent can call. Programmatic integration with the
Calypso platform is delivered to licensed customers through platform interfaces
described in customer-only documentation.
